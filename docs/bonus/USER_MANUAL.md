# 📄 User Manual
**Guide for Worshippers & Community Members - BIN FAIZAL'S Mosque Services PWA**

---

## 1. Accessing & Installing the App

1. Open your browser and navigate to the mosque web portal.
2. Tap the **"Install App"** banner or select **Add to Home Screen** from browser options.
3. Once installed, launch the app directly from your home screen even when offline.

---

## 2. Using Features

- **Prayer Times**: View today's Azan and Iqamah schedules.
- **Qibla Compass**: Grant location access and hold your phone flat to point toward Makkah.
- **Daily Content**: Swipe between Daily Verse, Hadith, and Duas.
- **Mosque News**: Enable notifications to receive funeral alerts and moon sighting announcements.
