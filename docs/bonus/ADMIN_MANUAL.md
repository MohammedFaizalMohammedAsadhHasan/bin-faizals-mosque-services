# 📄 Administrator Manual
**Guide for Imams, Board Members & Mosque Staff - BIN FAIZAL'S Mosque Services**

---

## 1. Logging into Admin Panel

1. Navigate to `/admin` in your browser.
2. Enter your authorized email address and password.
3. Complete 2FA OTP verification if enabled.

---

## 2. Managing Monthly Prayer Timetables

1. Click on **Prayer Timetable** in the left sidebar menu.
2. Click **Upload CSV** and select your formatted timetable file.
3. Review the preview table and click **Confirm & Import**.
4. To edit a single day, click the calendar cell and update Azan or Iqamah delay.

---

## 3. Triggering Emergency Broadcasts

1. Click the red **Emergency Alert** button on the top right header.
2. Type your message (e.g., "Janazah Solat after Asr today").
3. Click **Broadcast to TV Screens**. All TV displays will instantly show the full-screen banner.
